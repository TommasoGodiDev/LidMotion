Frequently Asked Questions

Does it drain my battery? ▼
Not at all. LidMotion uses a highly optimized hardware event listener. It registers 0.0% CPU usage when idle and only wakes up the GPU for the fraction of a second when the lid is actively moving.
Can I use it with an external monitor? ▼
Yes! LidMotion only renders on your Mac's built-in display. If you close your MacBook to use an external monitor in "Clamshell Mode", the animation will play perfectly on the built-in screen just before it shuts off.
Why does it need Screen Recording permissions? ▼
To create the seamless 3D folding effect, LidMotion needs to capture a single frame of your desktop exactly when you start closing the lid. This frame is rendered in 3D space. No data is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted.
